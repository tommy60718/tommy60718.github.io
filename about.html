About Me

I'm Lin Yang Sen, a music enthusiast and developer. This website serves as both my personal space to document my musical journey and a platform to explore the connection between music, mood, and creativity.

This project combines my passion for music with my interest in web development. The Music & Mood Station features a custom-built music player with mood-based visualizations, a collection of guitar learning resources, and a blog where I share my thoughts and experiences with music.

When I'm not coding or working on music projects, I enjoy playing guitar, swimming, and reading about music theory and technology. I believe in the power of music to enhance our lives and I'm constantly exploring new ways to connect technology with musical expression.
